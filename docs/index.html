What the app does

Five tabs: Status, Battery, Monitoring, Devices, Settings.

Live battery gauge

Level, charging state, temperature, voltage and current from Android's own battery data, refreshed every second while the app is open.

Available

Charging power and speed

Shows the power going into the battery (volts x amps) and labels it Slow, Normal, Fast, Super Fast or Ultra Fast. This is what the battery receives, not the charger's printed rating.

AvailableReal-device validation pending

Time to full / empty

Estimates come from how your battery level actually moves over time. With too little data, it shows nothing instead of guessing.

Available

Charging history

Each charging session is recorded: start and end time, battery level, peak temperature and average power.

Available

Heat awareness

Uses your region and local weather to tell whether a warm battery is just a hot day or something your phone is doing.

Available

Bluetooth device battery

See connected earbuds and other Bluetooth devices. A battery percentage is shown only when your phone and the device report one. Some phones never do, and then the app says "Battery: Unavailable".

Available

Voice alerts with quiet hours

Spoken alerts for charger and battery events. Routine alerts stay quiet from 11 PM to 6 AM; critical heat alerts still come through. Your music is paused for an alert and resumed only if the app paused it.

Available

Which apps use power

Per-app usage from Android's Usage Access, so you can see what is draining your phone.

Available

Reports you can keep

Export recorded readings and charging sessions to PDF or CSV. Values the phone cannot measure are marked unavailable in the report.

Available

Safe updates

The app checks GitHub Releases for new versions, checks the file's checksum and signature, and only installs after you confirm.

Available

What you get out of it

Plain benefits, no hype.

Trust the numbers

Everything on screen comes from Android. When Android gives nothing, you see "Unavailable", not a fake zero.

Spot slow chargers and bad cables

The charging tier tells you at a glance if a charger or cable is giving your phone less power than you expect.

Catch overheating early

Temperature tracking and heat context help you notice when your phone runs hotter than it should.

Easy on your battery

Built to use very little power itself. The one-second live refresh runs only while the app is on screen.

Your data stays on your phone

Readings and history are stored on the device. Crash reports are only sent if a reporting server is set up, which is not the case today.

If an install falls 5 or more versions behind the latest release, the app asks for the update before it can be used again, for security.

Microphone privacy

An optional switch that mutes the microphone when the screen is off, and never touches calls or your own manual mute. It tells you if the phone does not allow it.

Planned

Privacy monitor

An alert when some app is using the microphone or camera. Android does not tell normal apps which app it is, so the app can only say that something is in use and point to Android's Privacy Dashboard.
