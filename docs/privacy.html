Privacy policy

Battery Sentinel Pro Netra (the app) and this website. Last updated: October 2, 2026. Developer contact: prayagideepak@gmail.com.

The app, in short

The app reads battery, charging, temperature, Bluetooth and similar status from Android on your phone to show it to you. This data stays on your phone. The app does not create an account, does not show ads and does not sell or share data.

Optional feedback and crash reports (sent only if you choose)

Before anything is sent, the app shows you exactly what will be sent and asks for your consent each time. Nothing is sent automatically. The complete list is:

  • Your phone model (for example, "Realme 9 Pro 5G")
  • Your Android version
  • The app version
  • For a crash report only: the technical stack trace of the crash (code locations and error type)
  • For feedback only: the message you type

We do not send your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, device identifiers (such as IMEI, advertising ID or serial number), Bluetooth device names or battery history. Reports are delivered to the developer's email inbox and are used only to fix bugs and improve the app. They are kept for as long as needed to fix the problem, then deleted. To ask for deletion, email the address above.

The website

  • Ratings, suggestions and complaints are public. What you type (stars or message, your phone model, Android version if given, and for ratings the name you choose or "Anonymous") is stored in Google Firebase and shown on the website. Please do not type personal details. You cannot edit an entry after sending; to have one removed, email the developer.
  • Replies from the team are public and signed "Netra by Prayagi Team".
  • Email address (optional) is sent privately to the developer to reply to you, and is never stored in the public database or shown.
  • Uploaded images or videos (optional, max 2 MB) are sent privately to the developer and never shown publicly.
  • Forms are delivered by the FormSubmit email service. Visits are counted with a free third-party counter. Downloads are counted by GitHub.

Permissions the app may ask for

Each permission is requested only for the feature that needs it, and that feature stops when you turn the permission off: notifications (alerts), Bluetooth (connected device list and battery), phone state (to avoid speaking over calls), usage access (which apps use power), and system settings or audio settings only for optional features you switch on. You can revoke any permission in Android settings.

Your choices and contact

You can use the app without ever sending feedback. To ask about, correct or delete something you sent, email prayagideepak@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
